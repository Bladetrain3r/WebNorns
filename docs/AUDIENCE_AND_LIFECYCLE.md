# Audience and lifecycle

*Brainstorm captured 2026-10-15 from Ziggy. Direction, not a spec.*

## The goal above the goal
Make A-Life that people want to keep and carry with them, on any device. The aim is to spark interest in artificial life through quality and warmth, not through pressure. Free, open source, zero install.

## Mortality is opt-in
- **Default: stasis, not death.** A neglected norn goes dormant (a cocoon or curled-up glow in the nest) and wakes when tended: groggy, a little behind, maybe missing some recent habits. A gentle consequence, never a loss. Nobody loses a norn without having chosen frailty.
- **Mortal norns are chosen, and better at something.** Picture elves and humans: a mortal norn learns faster, heals faster, grows faster, and passes more learning to its offspring (ties into the Norn Ranch work on inheritance). Its memorial carries weight because the choice was made.
- Mortality is a per-norn or per-lineage choice, shown plainly at hatch and changeable only in the direction the design allows (decide: can a mortal norn be made immortal later?).
- The rule still holds: touch knobs, never pin values. Stasis and mortality change how the body's chemistry behaves; they are not UI switches that set drives.

## Bookmark your norn
- The norn lives in the URL: genome, traits and a compact state encoded in the fragment (`#...`), so a bookmark or a shared link carries the norn. No server, no account.
- An optional short seed code (a few words) for typing or reading out loud.
- Local saving stays the primary store; the link is the portable copy. Decide how a link held by two browsers diverges (forks are fine and on theme).

## Landing page
- A live norn already pottering about, above the fold. Show, don't explain.
- One "Hatch your own" action. One honest line about what this is and that it is free. Repo link.
- Works on a phone. Loads fast. No sign-up, no tracking.

## Open questions
- Stasis cues: how soon, how visible, and what is lost.
- Exact advantages of mortality, and how to balance them.
- URL size limits for state, and what goes in the fragment versus local storage.
